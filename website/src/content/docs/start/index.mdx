ReviewStage is the human review layer for AI-written pull requests. Claude drafts the review from the real diff and stages it for you. You tick the findings worth posting and post them under your own GitHub name. Nothing reaches GitHub until you click.

```bash
npx reviewstage
```

## What you get

- A **queue** of the PRs waiting for your review, on your laptop and on your phone.
- A **drafted review** for each one: a reading guide, an assessment, then findings with file and line, every one editable. Each finding says where to look and what to check, and carries a Checked, Likely or Maybe badge earned from a local evidence check and a second disproof pass.
- **Post** as a plain `COMMENT` review, as you. **Approve** is a separate click.
- A **learnings loop**: what you drop or reword shapes the next review of that repository.
- Reviews run on **your own Claude plan**, through [Claude Code](https://docs.anthropic.com/en/docs/claude-code), only when you click.

It is a reviewer's assistant, not a review bot. Five facts hold at every size:

- The draft is private before publication.
- You choose what is correct and worth saying.
- The review posts under your own GitHub identity.
- Approval is always a separate click of yours.
- The drafting agent cannot write to GitHub: it runs with no GitHub credentials, its write tools are denied, and the PR's reviews, comments and threads are fingerprinted before and after the run.

It never posts on its own, never posts as a bot, and never requests changes or approves on its own. The agent can only produce a file; every write to GitHub is your click with your token.
