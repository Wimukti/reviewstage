ReviewStage — Claude drafts your PR review, you post it as yourself

**Description:**

AI wrote the PR. You still have to review it.

ReviewStage drafts your pull-request review from the real diff, on your own Claude plan, and
stages every finding privately. You keep what's worth saying, edit it, and post it as a normal
review comment under your own GitHub name. Nothing posts until you click.

Try it: npx reviewstage
Website: https://reviewstage.dev
Code (MIT): https://github.com/Wimukti/reviewstage

• One command opens a desktop app (Mac and Linux)
• Your phone joins by scanning a QR code; push notifications for review requests
• Swipe right to keep a finding, left to drop it, then Post
• It learns what your team drops and proposes rules you approve
• The review step has no GitHub write path — enforced, not promised
